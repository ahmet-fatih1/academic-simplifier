# Technical Decisions

This document records important technical decisions to preserve consistency over time.

---

## Framework

### Decision

Next.js (Pages Router)

### Reason

Simple, stable, and well suited for the current project size.

---

## Backend

### Decision

Next.js API Routes

### Reason

Keeps frontend and backend together and simplifies deployment.

---

## AI Provider

### Decision

Google Gemini API

### Reason

Good balance between quality, cost, and ease of integration.

---

## Deployment

### Decision

Vercel

### Reason

Easy deployment and native Next.js support.

---

## General Philosophy

When multiple solutions exist, prefer the one that is:

1. Simpler
2. Easier to maintain
3. More secure
4. Easier to understand
