# Simplify My Paper

## Overview

Simplify My Paper is an AI-powered web application that generates clear and concise summaries of academic papers.

The goal is to make research papers easier to understand while preserving the original meaning.

---

## Vision

Build a fast, simple, and reliable platform that helps students, researchers, and curious learners understand scientific literature.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Researchers
- Academics
- Lifelong learners

---
